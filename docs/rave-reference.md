# The Rave / Eagles Club

The Rave is a district landmark, tour stop and walking destination. `rave-review.html` uses the same model and shipped terrain as the city, with views of each elevation and day, sunset and night lighting.

## Reference evidence

The supplied pack's `re_01` and `re_04` show the same front oblique exterior. `re_05` shows the central arches and upper arcade. Files `re_02` and `re_03` are interior photographs despite their exterior filenames; model decisions follow image contents.

The [Wisconsin Historical Society property record](https://www.wisconsinhistory.org/Records/Property/HI17154) confirms the north entrance pavilion, three tall arches, upper balustraded arcade, ornamental side wings and paired side windows. The [official venue page](https://www.therave.com/ourvenues.asp) supplies current venue context. Permanent architecture is modeled with original geometry and lettering; the seasonal decorations and concert photographs are not included as textures.

## Site and interpretation

OSM way 567330938 supplies the venue footprint, about 45 by 72 metres. Exact source matching removes its former building extrusion at both tile levels while preserving roads and nearby buildings.

The Wisconsin Avenue sidewalk is higher than the building-centre terrain. The model uses an entrance datum and a graded approach; the foundation extends to the lower rear terrain. The exterior detailing, height and lighting are photo-based approximations. Public interior photographs inform the building's volumes but this is not a navigable interior reconstruction.

Validation covers finite geometry, entrance visibility and walking access on shipped road/terrain geometry, lighting, sign orientation, and source-specific replacement. Browser inspection checks the modeled facade and ground contact separately from those tests.
