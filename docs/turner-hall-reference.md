# Turner Hall Ballroom exterior

The production district and `/turner-hall-review.html` share `buildTurnerHall`. The landmark menu, fly-through and walking destinations include Turner Hall. Day/sunset/night presets control its windows and entrance lanterns.

## Evidence and orientation

- [National Park Service nomination](https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/GetAsset/NHLS/77000041_text): 100 × 150 ft main building, west-facing ornamental elevation, north addition, limestone base, cream/red brick, twin gables and central dormered tower.
- [Milwaukee Turners](https://www.milwaukeeturners.org/turner-ballroom): venue identity and ballroom history.
- User-supplied `turner_hall_threejs_reference_pack/photos_current`: front facade, southwest oblique and south fire-escape views provide window composition, roof and entrance details. The photographs show the arched entrance; this takes precedence over the older nomination's account of a 1958 alteration.
- Cached OSM way 69298480, extracted September 6, 2026: mapped footprint and street placement. Local −X faces Vel R. Phillips Avenue; +Z points south. The original packed placeholder is removed by its exact node/elevation signature in both detail levels, preserving neighbors.

The model is an interpreted exterior, not a measured survey. Heights, window dimensions, roof pitch, rear fenestration and service stairs are estimated from the photographs. The north addition is simplified; the OSM outline does not resolve separate roof volumes. Fine masonry uses original procedural geometry. Reference photos, concert artwork and commercial logos are not included in distributed assets. Turner Hall Ballroom is identified by ordinary text in the district UI.

## Verification

Geometry checks cover the roof/material merge, finite vertices, terrain contact, west-facing orientation and lighting transitions. Cached-tile tests verify that only the original building triangles are removed, in both full and reduced-detail tiles. Browser review covers the west elevation, southwest corner and sunset lighting; the dedicated review provides all-side and pedestrian camera presets.
