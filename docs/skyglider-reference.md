# Summerfest Skyglider

The Skyglider is a north–south circulating chairlift above the festival's main walkway. The outbound and return lanes turn around at the two terminals; the route does not circle the perimeter of the grounds.

## Evidence and placement

- [Summerfest's official activities page](https://www.summerfest.com/summerfest-activities/) places it above the main walkway and describes one-way and round-trip rides.
- The [Wisconsin Historical Society's north terminal record](https://www.wisconsinhistory.org/record/property/HI225451/redirect) describes the multicolored chairlift traveling north–south between its terminals.
- The cached September 6, 2026 OSM extract contains way **597941283**. Its north endpoint is **43.0341233, -87.8989875**, scene **X489.2245/Z96.9402**; its south endpoint is **43.0297648, -87.8986992**, scene **X512.6829/Z578.8770**. The mapped polyline is approximately **482.8 m** long.
- The source intermediate points are **491.5679/159.7020**, **490.3718/267.6665**, and **493.9764/304.4102**. They guide placement over the walkway, but are not tagged towers or evidence of engineered cable turns. A straight endpoint shortcut would intersect an existing concession footprint; the source alignment preserves that building.
- The [airport's June 2022 release](https://www.mitchellairport.com/application/files/8816/5419/4257/MKEAirportSkygliderSponsorship.pdf) documents **94 cars**. The model uses that count for density; it is not a separately verified 2026 inventory.
- The supplied [Lift Blog photographs](https://liftblog.com/sky-glider-summerfest-wi/) show red cylindrical towers, black curved hangers, white seat interiors, and blue/red/yellow/green open two-person chairs with matching rounded canopies. The model uses original geometry, without downloaded photographic textures.

## Estimates, not measurements

No reliable published numerical ride height or tower survey was found. Photo scale suggests approximately **10–12 m cable height above grade** along the elevated section and **4–5 m near the terminals**. The model uses an approximately **11 m main cable height**, with low boarding terminals and near-ground seats. Chair dimensions, tower spacing, lane separation, span sag, and animation speed are visual estimates. The reference pack's generic 31 m vertical profile, placeholder coordinates, four-seat carrier, and 0.72 m lane separation are not used as factual dimensions.

The chairs follow a continuous closed path at an illustrative steady speed. They stay upright and face the direction of travel through the terminal turns. The review provides a pause/play control; the main city respects reduced-motion preferences. Warm terminal illumination follows sunset/night mode. One procedural tree intersecting the mapped chair corridor is excluded; existing mapped building footprints are retained.
