About this miniature

A simplified 3D model of Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Buildings are extruded from real footprints; heights come from OpenStreetMap tags where available and documented estimates otherwise. A few landmarks are hand-modeled interpretations, not measured models.

Sources

  • Buildings, roads, parks, coastline and water: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
  • City limits and river polygons: City of Milwaukee Open Data, CC-BY.
  • Streetcar: The Hop / City of Milwaukee GTFS snapshot supplied September 6, 2026; track alignment © OpenStreetMap contributors. Scheduled preview with estimated motion and platform details; no live vehicle locations.
  • Elevation: Terrain Tiles on AWS.

Built with Three.js. Source and pipeline details are in the project README.
